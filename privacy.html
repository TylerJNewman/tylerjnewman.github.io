OpenClaw privacy policy

OpenClaw is a personal tool. Only Tyler Newman uses it, with his own Google account.

It accesses the Google account data he authorizes, on his behalf, for his own tasks. That can include passing the data to software and AI services he chooses to use.

It does not collect data from anyone else, sell data, or use data for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.
